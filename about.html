About Me

As a designer, photographer, and educator, I see myself as an explorer—an observer responding to the world around me, both physically and intellectually. I experiment with traditional and contemporary media, driven by a need for change, discovery, and the excitement of creative problem-solving.

My design practice is rooted in the poster as a powerful medium for social engagement and cultural commentary. I am equally drawn to sustainable design, creating objects that emerge from natural or reclaimed materials and reflect an awareness of environmental responsibility. I value process as much as outcome: sketching remains an essential part of my daily routine. These sketches often evolve digitally—refined, reinterpreted, or sometimes left raw to preserve a handmade aesthetic and emotional authenticity.

My photography centers on the conceptual, exploring the dialogue between light and shadow, chance and intention. Whether capturing the spontaneity of the street or composing a deliberate visual essay, I strive to see with the eyes of a child—or a traveler discovering the familiar anew.

As an educator, I am continually inspired by the shared learning process with my students. I value exploring subjects from fresh angles and witnessing the spark of creativity take hold. There is no greater reward than seeing that "eureka moment" in someone else's eyes.
